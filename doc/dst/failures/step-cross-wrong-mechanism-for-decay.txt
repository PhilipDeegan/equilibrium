CONTEXT: After gate tuning (v1 vs barrier, any m0) repeatedly failed to produce a "neutron sheds circulation, converges to a locked proton" decay signature, checked doc/dst.md directly to settle whether step_cross is even the right mechanism family for decay at all.

WHAT FAILED: Every step_cross-driven "decay"/"neutron sheds circulation" test in this project (the lockstep/barrier-sweep work, and by extension project_step_cross_v2/project_spheromak_decay's whole line) turns out to have applied the wrong operator family, not just the wrong gate or calibration.

ROOT CAUSE: doc/dst.md draws a hard structural line between two propagation modes in its "Inertia and the Face/Cell Gate" section: face-hopping (phi moves between adjacent cells across shared faces — the natural, low-cost mode; EM waves, gravitational waves, and u-field gradients all use this) versus through-cell crossing (phi must cross a cell's own interior — harder by construction, the coupling step_cross implements, existing ONLY to let a closed topological winding translate rigidly through the lattice as a collective all-or-nothing threshold event, and explicitly the SOURCE of inertial mass). Decay is specified in a completely separate section ("Decay rate = distance from nearest resonance") with NO reference to crossing at all — an off-resonance lambda "cannot close perfectly; a small persistent phi imbalance bleeds out AS RADIATION (neutrinos) tick by tick," i.e. via the cheap face-hopping channel, not step_cross's expensive through-cell-crossing channel. This directly explains why gate tuning could only ever interpolate between "erode like v1" and "freeze completely" — neither endpoint was ever going to look like resonance-driven radiative decay, because that's not what step_cross represents; it's DST's inertia/translation mechanism.

LESSON: step_cross should be set aside for any future decay work and reserved strictly for translation/inertia questions. A genuinely theory-consistent decay probe must use plain step() radiative face-hopping and be framed around resonance-distance-from-a-BCC-topological-eigenstate (doc/dst.md's actual named mechanism, ~line 544: "compute the resonant lambda spectrum... lifetimes scaling as distance from the nearest eigenstate"), not step_cross erosion under any gate. The open/closed-knot radiation probe (plain step() only, no step_cross) is the one decay-relevant result in this project's history that's actually theory-consistent so far.

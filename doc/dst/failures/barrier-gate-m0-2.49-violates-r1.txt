CONTEXT: the electron/positron scripts (isolated_dipole.py, electron_positron_collision.py) defaulted to gate="barrier", m0=2.49 — a value taken from doc/dst_next.md's derivation for the NEUTRON's real radius (m0 = scaling_factor x m_p/m_Planck), never intended for the electron.

WHAT FAILED: isolated electron under m0=2.49 erodes to surv~0.879 by step 1500 (then plateaus) — R1 (electron double-ring must not erode) is badly broken under this "default" gate value, on every script using it. This retroactively invalidates any earlier finding that used m0=2.49 electron/positron pairs and reported reconnection/current-sheet/oscillating-survival behavior as genuine two-body physics — a direct real-pair measurement at m0=12 (which does freeze the electron) showed the pair stays perfectly frozen (surv_e=surv_p=1.0000 throughout), proving those earlier findings were self-erosion artifacts, not interaction physics.

ROOT CAUSE: raw_barrier_drive (Sum_k W_k*max(-phi,0), pure local magnitude) can't distinguish a healthy stationary winding's own ordinary rotation from genuine translation-driving asymmetry — it fires on an isolated, non-translating structure just from its normal internal circulation. Measured directly: raw_barrier_drive on the electron's own IC (scale=8: max=8.42, 41% of inner cells >2.49) substantially overlaps the neutron's actual Taylor-spheromak IC distribution (scale=4.8: max=7.64, median=2.34, 48% >2.49) — no m0 threshold can cleanly separate the two populations by magnitude alone. Confirmed: m0=12 fixes R1 but the neutron then shows 0% firing (R2 fails completely, not just slower).

LESSON: never use gate="barrier", m0=2.49 (or any single m0) as a "safe default" for the electron double-ring — it erodes it. Use gate="opposed", ref=50 instead (direction-comparison to neighbour's transit vector, o=max(0,-v_i.v_nb)) — it's exactly zero on ordinary internal rotation and only fires on genuine >90 degree reversal, and was confirmed to cleanly freeze the isolated electron (surv 1.0000->1.0001 over 2000 steps). Any prior result built on a barrier/m0=2.49 electron-positron pair should be treated as contaminated by self-erosion until re-run under a gate that actually passes R1.

CONTEXT: A C++ synthetic test (tst/eq/core/data/lattice/test_step_cross_per_level.cpp,
added 2026-08-13) tried to measure step_cross's per-level rate using a
perfectly uniform phi field on every face.

WHAT FAILED: The test did not measure what it claimed to. It was deleted
2026-08-15 rather than fixed, after mechanism-verifying (not just guessing)
why it was invalid.

ROOT CAUSE: _step_cross_local's redistribute-to-adjacent-faces design makes
a perfectly uniform field self-cancel almost completely in the interior of
the domain. So the test actually measured "what fraction of a small
sphere's cells sit on the boundary" — not step_cross's per-level rate at
all. Not representative of anything.

LESSON: Don't build a step_cross rate test on a spatially uniform phi IC —
the redistribute-to-adjacent-faces mechanism cancels a uniform field out in
bulk, so only boundary-cell artifacts survive. Use a genuinely varying/
gradient field so interior faces carry real signal. Note: step_cross's own
per-level-rate question remains genuinely open, separately (see
tst/substrate/adaptive/test_step_cross_per_level.py and
[[project_step_cross_v2]] memory) — it was never actually tested by this
now-deleted synthetic test.

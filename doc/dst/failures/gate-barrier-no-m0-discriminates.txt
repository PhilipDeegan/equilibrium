CONTEXT: Re-tested the lockstep/free-energy (circulation-erosion) diagnostic under gate="barrier" (the theory-consistent, all-or-nothing threshold gate) instead of the default v1 (continuous diffusion gate), using a derived m0=1.494 (rescaled from doc/dst_next.md §8's neutron calibration), to see if the theory-consistent gate rescues the "neutron sheds circulation, converges to a locked proton" hypothesis that v1 failed to show.

WHAT FAILED: barrier tracked v1 almost exactly (circulation decay 0.8183 vs 0.8187 by step 6000, swing -0.433 vs -0.404) — not a different qualitative shape. A follow-up m0 sweep (1.494 / 2.49 / 4.46(median) / 7 / 9.38(max) / 12 / 20, 2000 steps each) showed firing fraction drops smoothly and monotonically from 87% down to 0%, and circulation-at-2000-steps tracks it exactly, from 0.935 (heavy erosion) up to 1.000007 (exact freeze) — a smooth one-parameter interpolation between the two already-known endpoints (v1-like continuous erosion / no crossing at all).

ROOT CAUSE: Measured the raw pooled drive Sum_k W_k*max(-phi_ik,0) directly on this IC — median nonzero-cell drive is 4.46, vs m0=1.494, and 87% of nonzero-drive cells exceed m0. The gate isn't discriminating anything on this IC — it fires almost everywhere regardless of m0 choice, including m0=12 (the historically-used "R1-preserving" value, which sits above this IC's max drive and freezes it completely instead). No m0 in the gate's own parameter space produces the qualitatively different shape (partial, self-limiting erosion) the "converges to a locked stable remainder" hypothesis needs.

LESSON: Don't re-test this lockstep hypothesis by sweeping m0 again on the same IC family (curl-full poloidal+toroidal spheromak) — it's been shown twice now (once via a single derived m0, once via a full sweep spanning below-median to above-max) that no m0 value produces partial convergence; the result is a genuine property of the gate-vs-IC-amplitude relationship, not a mistuned constant. This is what motivated questioning step_cross's ontology itself instead — see step-cross-wrong-mechanism-for-decay.txt.

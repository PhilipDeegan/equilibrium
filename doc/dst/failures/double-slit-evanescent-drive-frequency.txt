CONTEXT: double_slit.py demo needs a continuously-driven quasi-monochromatic
source so enough cycles reach the screen to form a fringe pattern (a single
Gaussian pulse doesn't carry enough cycles).

WHAT FAILED: first choice of drive period was period_steps=24 (omega~a much
higher frequency). The wave decayed near the source instead of propagating
to the backplate -- no fringe pattern could form.

ROOT CAUSE: period_steps=24 put the drive frequency above the lattice's
dispersion cutoff (omega_max ~ 12.5 for W_HEX=3/W_SQ=1). Above cutoff, modes
are evanescent (spatially decaying, not propagating), not photon-like waves.

LESSON: before choosing a drive period/frequency for any oscillator source
on this lattice, check it against the dispersion cutoff omega_max for the
weight configuration in use (W_HEX/W_SQ dependent) -- an evanescent choice
looks like a near-field decay artefact, not an obviously-wrong number.
Working default that stayed safely inside the propagating band:
drive_period_steps=250 (omega~4.9, wavelength~4-5 cells).

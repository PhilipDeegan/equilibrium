CONTEXT: Several times while hunting for DST's missing Ampère/Lorentz coupling, a classical/macroscopic mechanism was reached for (wire+conductor equations, MHD flux-freezing, a Yee/FIT/DEC-style primal/dual mesh, a chiral lam-based Ampère formula) and Philip corrected each as a category error — borrowing structure DST hasn't validated or doesn't need.

WHAT FAILED, one by one:
(1) "A wire implies protons and electrons." Explaining the missing coupling via del^2 A = -mu0*J (the classical bulk-conductor equation), or via set_flux_loop's "frozen battery current" construction, presupposes an entire macroscopic assembly (stationary ions + mobile free charge) that DST has never validated as existing or emergent.
(2) "Lam doesn't cycle, and lam requires protons/mass." Proposed sourcing phi circulation from a lam-based term, reasoning lam was "depleted flux already moving circularly at c." Wrong on both counts: lam is a bookkeeping/depletion trace, not a cycling quantity, and it's only meaningful where a proton-like structure already exists — nothing to source from in bare vacuum.
(3) "DST is missing a Yee/FIT/DEC-style primal/dual mesh pair." The primal/dual split in those classical schemes is a representational device for the Hodge-star relationship between complementary-degree forms, not a metaphysical requirement. DST's Kelvin cell already has edges as real geometry (the 12-triangle plaquettes are built from them) — no new mesh needed.
(4) A postulated chiral/directional Ampère formula, Gamma_L = mu0*lam*c/ell, making lam itself carry a direction. Wrong: lam is the throughput modifier (sigma=1/(1+lam)), radial+scalar by design, not directional. Phi itself is already the moving flux/current and already sources field geometry via curl(phi)=B — no separate directional current-source term is needed or valid.

ROOT CAUSE: Each case imported a piece of classical continuum electromagnetism or MHD machinery wholesale instead of grounding the mechanism in DST's actual discrete primitives (u, phi, lam, the 12-triangle plaquette). Classical intuition about what "current," "conductor," "mesh," or "chirality" require doesn't transfer without re-deriving from the substrate rules.

LESSON: Before proposing any missing-coupling mechanism, check whether it presupposes a macroscopic assembly (wire/conductor/plasma) DST hasn't built, or bolts on a classical mesh/formula rather than using DST's own edge/plaquette geometry. Ground new mechanisms in u/phi/lam directly; re-derive, don't borrow.

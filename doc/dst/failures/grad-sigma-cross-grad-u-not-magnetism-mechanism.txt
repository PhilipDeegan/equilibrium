CONTEXT: a derived formula, dCirc/dt = -dt*W_sq*d2^2*(grad(sigma) x
grad(u)) — a Biermann-battery-like "electric<->magnetic leak"
identity — was proposed in-session as a mechanism for how DST
magnetism arises from charge-like sources.

WHAT FAILED: the formula was DEPRECATED as a magnetism-mechanism
claim; it does not describe how DST magnetism actually works.

ROOT CAUSE: it conflated charge-like u/lam sources with
motion/circulation-like magnetism — a category error. DST magnetism
is understood elsewhere (see [[project_dst_magnetic_field]]) as
arising from motion/circulation — u-translation of rotating lam
producing a phi wake — not from a static gradient-cross-gradient
source term between sigma and u.

LESSON: the algebra itself is still valid for the one narrow question
it actually answers (can a lam-gradient generate net circulation from
none — yes, in principle) and, per
[[project_curl_full_dipole_spheromak]], is real but numerically
inaccessible at any real scale. Don't re-derive it, and don't cite it
as "how DST magnetism works" — that specific interpretive claim is
retracted; the algebra is not.

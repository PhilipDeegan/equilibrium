CONTEXT: tested the framing "proton = poloidal+toroidal locked together (stable); neutron = same plus extra circulation as free energy, released by step_cross's decay, converging toward a locked proton remnant" (Philip's live hypothesis, 2026-09-02).

WHAT FAILED: built the curl-full dipole (poloidal+toroidal) on a flat DampedLattice (R=30, r_sph=8, N=147,047), tracked u_rms (poloidal tracer) and macro shell circulation (toroidal tracer) for 6000 steps under (a) plain step() and (b) step()+step_cross(scale=1e-3). Script: exp/2026/09/02/23_00_lockstep_free_energy/probe_lockstep_free_energy.py. With step_cross active, circulation stops being frozen and decays monotonically (1.000→0.818 by step 6000, ~18%), while u_rms keeps oscillating with essentially the SAME amplitude/character as the no-cross run — not damped toward any converged value. The swing := u_ratio - circ_ratio metric does not shrink toward zero with cross on (final -0.433 vs -0.575 without cross — same order of magnitude, no convergence).

ROOT CAUSE (diagnosed): what step_cross does here looks like the same generic circulation erosion already documented elsewhere in this project (e.g. ring circulation tests), not evidence of convergence toward any specific locked/stable configuration. Bigger caveat: the test used gate="v1" (the default), which is flagged in TWO existing memories as suspect for exactly this kind of use. [[project_diffusion_vs_gate_mismatch]]: doc/dst.md's own theory says crossing/translation is a discrete all-or-nothing THRESHOLD gate, not continuous diffusion — but gate="v1" is literally delta=-dt·scale·sigma²·inward, textbook linear/Fickian relaxation, structurally the opposite of the theory doc's description; gate="barrier" (the theory-matching alternative) has never been carried forward into this project's cross_scale-dependent findings. [[project_step_cross_v2]]: separately, an R1/R2 audit found v1 "PROVEN inverted" on other tests (wrong direction), no gate passing both required criteria, barrier never properly tested either. So this finding may not be evidence about decay at all — it may just be evidence that v1-diffusion is doing something unrelated to whatever step_cross is supposed to represent.

LESSON: the underlying physical picture (proton = locked two-flux-tube structure, neutron = proton + extra circulation as free energy) is NOT ruled out by this test — only this particular mechanism/diagnostic (step_cross driving swing→0 under gate="v1") failed to confirm it. Do not treat any step_cross-based "decay" result (this one or earlier ones) as validated physics until the gate ontology question is resolved. Before retrying: re-test with gate="barrier" (not guaranteed correct, just theory-consistent instead of admittedly-diffusion-shaped) — and treat whether step_cross as a whole is even the right ontology for "decay"/resistivity as a genuinely open question, not a settled premise. A cleaner diagnostic that separates real secular erosion from the u-oscillation's own periodicity (cycle-averaged u_rms vs. circulation decay rate) has also not yet been tried.

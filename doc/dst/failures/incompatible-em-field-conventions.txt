CONTEXT: across sessions, three different E/B "conventions" were used
to try to build a 3D Poynting/energy-flux vector S=ExB in DST, in
order to validate radiated-energy predictions (Hertzian dipole,
Poynting-ring far-zone). They contradict each other.

WHAT FAILED (three incompatible conventions found, no single one
validated):
1. test_maxwell.py's convention treats u as literally "a scalar proxy
   for the electric field COMPONENT" — rigorously tested to 12
   decimals, but only for a 1D/plane-wave dispersion check. Never
   designed to build a genuine 3D S for an arbitrary source.
2. The Poynting-ring probes' convention, E:=-dphi/dt, B:=curl(phi),
   used for months including all Hertzian-dipole attempts, failed
   three independent checks under the exact same formula (see
   poynting-local-theorem-check-fails.txt and
   hertzian-dipole-wrong-angular-ratio.txt).
3. An older session's note (doc/dst_next.md sec 5) used the full
   4-potential E:=-grad(u)-dphi/dt, which is confirmed identically
   zero under plain step() (a real, solid algebraic identity, kept as
   SOLID in the standing notes) — and from that concluded step_cross
   is REQUIRED to source any E at all. That is the OPPOSITE conclusion
   from the same overall investigation's "drop step_cross" fix to the
   Poynting-ring equator/pole result, which used convention #2.

ROOT CAUSE: not diagnosed/resolved. Open question left standing: did
dropping step_cross for the Poynting-ring equator/pole result
(R=30/R=50, ratio ~0.86-0.99) fix something real, or was it an
artifact of convention #2, which has since failed three separate
checks using that exact same formula? The traveling-wave reproducer's
success (shift-match correlation 0.97-0.99, a genuinely positive
result) does NOT validate convention #2 either — that test compared
the whole (u,phi) state directly, never split it into E/B or computed
S at all. It's fully possible u/phi genuinely co-propagate correctly
while every attempt to extract a meaningful S=ExB from them (any of
the 3 conventions) remains wrong.

LESSON: do not cite any of the three conventions as settled. Do not
pick a "4th guessed convention" either. Before doing more Poynting/
energy-flux work, derive the flux J that satisfies dH_i/dt =
(discrete divergence of J)_i EXACTLY from the update rule (the same
rigorous symbolic method used for the traveling-wave companion phi
and the E_full≡0 identity), with proper staggered/energy-at-cells-
flux-at-faces bookkeeping. Only once that exact identity is found
should "drop step_cross" and the Hertzian-dipole/Poynting-ring checks
be revisited.

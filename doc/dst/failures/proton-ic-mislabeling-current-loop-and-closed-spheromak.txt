CONTEXT: on 2026-09-02, two separate hand-built IC objects were each provisionally called "the proton test" within the same session, and each was caught and corrected before the mislabeling propagated further.

WHAT FAILED (mislabeling #1): the curl-full dipole/spheromak IC (equilibrium/substrate/curl_full_dipole_ic.py::seed_curl_full_dipole_ic) — poloidal+toroidal Taylor state confined into a self-contained force-free equilibrium — was being treated as a proton candidate. Philip corrected this: per [[project_dst_neutron]], a closed knot (toroidal winding, zero net outward flux, charge-neutral by topology) is the NEUTRON signature, not the proton's. The proton is the OPEN version: poloidal+toroidal flux tubes whose u-flux genuinely radiates outward through all 14 faces (real long-range field / charge), not a confined structure.

WHAT FAILED (mislabeling #2): follow-up work then validated DST's discrete curl(phi)=B mechanism against a classical circular current loop (seeded phi from the exact analytic loop vector potential, reconstructed B, compared to the closed-form loop B field — clean match, see the positive curl-loop validation result). This was initially framed as "the proton test" too. Corrected again: a current loop has real nonzero curl by design — it is a magnetic-dipole/current-loop object, the OPPOSITE of what a proton needs. The proton candidate must be CURL-FREE (phi=grad(U)), matching "open knot: u-flux radiates outward — creates long-range field (charge)" far better than a circulating loop does.

ROOT CAUSE: both mistakes came from conflating "has an open/dipole-shaped far field" with "is the proton." A structure can look dipole-like or radiate a field while still being the wrong topological class (confined closed-loop circulation vs. genuine irrotational outward flux). The actual proton candidate identified after both corrections is equilibrium/substrate/dipole_mode_ic.py::seed_dipole_ic — poloidal-only, phi=grad(U), curl-free by construction, already confirmed exactly-zero macro circulation ([[project_curl_full_dipole_spheromak]] / [[project_dst_discrete_curl]]).

LESSON: before calling any hand-built IC "the proton," check two things explicitly: (1) is it a CLOSED structure (zero net outward flux, toroidal winding present) — if so it's a neutron candidate, not proton; (2) does it have real nonzero curl by construction (a current-loop-like circulation) — if so it's a magnetic-dipole/current object, a distinct property from charge, not the proton's monopole-like open structure either. Only a curl-free, poloidal-only, genuinely open (nonzero net outward u-flux through the domain boundary) structure qualifies. The curl-loop validation itself is NOT wasted — it stands as a general validation of DST's curl(phi)=B/Ampère mechanism, and may be relevant to a future proton "magnetic moment" property distinct from its charge — just don't call it the proton test. Standing caveat: every hand-built IC in this project remains preliminary since DST has no independent ground truth for what a real proton structure is.

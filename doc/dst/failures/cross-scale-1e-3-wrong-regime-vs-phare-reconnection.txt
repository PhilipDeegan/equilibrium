CONTEXT: DST's curl-full dipole spheromak was stepped 200 steps at `cross_scale=1e-3` (default `gate="v1"`) on both production checkpoints (uniform r180, L1R60 wider-margin); H/H0 stayed stable (uniform: 0.999→0.9979; L1R60: 0.9996→0.9993) and plots showed "no visible merging" through step 200. This was then compared against a first real quantitative read of PHARE's `~/tmp/EM_B.vtkhdf` reconnection data.

WHAT FAILED: The "no visible merging through step 200" reading does not mean what it appeared to mean. PHARE's own field data revealed a two-timescale relaxation: a FAST phase (t=0→~1.8) where `Bx_rms` (toroidal-like) collapses 0.136→0.011 (~92% gone — almost certainly the real reconnection/annihilation event, previously only eyeballed at a few snapshots), followed by a SLOW phase (t≈1.8→3.4) where `Bx_rms` plateaus (only ~5% further loss) while the in-plane (poloidal-like) component keeps decaying smoothly throughout. Whole-slice field energy dropped 26.93→10.30 (−62%), almost all of it in the fast phase. DST's own run lost <0.3% of H over its entire 200-step run — a ~100x+ weaker erosion rate than PHARE's fast phase alone.

ROOT CAUSE: DST's "no merging" conclusion was reached entirely inside what looks like PHARE's SLOW/plateau-regime analogue. DST has never been run anywhere near a `cross_scale` strong enough to probe PHARE's reconnection-dominated fast regime, so the comparison says nothing about whether DST's mechanism can or can't reproduce fast reconnection — the test was simply run in the wrong regime. Additionally, H/H0 is likely the wrong diagnostic for "has merging happened" at all: a real topology change can be small as a fraction of total H while huge as a fraction of one component (toroidal vs poloidal split), exactly as PHARE's Bx_rms showed.

LESSON: before drawing a "no merging" conclusion from a cross_scale run, make sure the regime being tested is comparable in strength to the reconnection-dominated regime of whatever benchmark it's being compared against. Track the toroidal/poloidal component split (or macro circulation) as the merging diagnostic, not H/H0. No unit-conversion between PHARE's `t` and DST's `cross_scale` has been attempted yet — needed before any of this can be made quantitative.

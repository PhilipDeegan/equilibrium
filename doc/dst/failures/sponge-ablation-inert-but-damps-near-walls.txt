CONTEXT: Before running the 2026-08-20 EM chirality verifiers, checked whether
the `DampedLattice` sponge BC (carried over from the prior day's traveling-wave
reproducer) mattered for these new single-wave-period tests
(exp/2026/08/20/17_21_sponge_ablation/).

WHAT FAILED: The sponge BC is provably inert for the metric it was validated
against (traveling-wave correlation: Δcorr<2e-3 with the sponge removed
entirely) but it DOES visibly damp the field near the domain walls within a
single wave period. sponge_ablation.png shows the sponge zone flattening to
~0 while the true IC tail there is ~0.3 amplitude — a large, visible effect
the correlation metric simply didn't register.

ROOT CAUSE: The sponge was reused unmodified from a different, longer-running
test where its near-wall damping was presumably harmless relative to that
test's timescale and observable. It was carried over into a new,
shorter/single-period test without re-checking that its side effects were
still negligible for what THIS test measures.

LESSON: A BC or mechanism shown "inert" for one summary statistic is not
inert in general — check its effect on the raw field, not just whatever
scalar metric it was validated against. For any future single-period
dispersion-style test, drop this sponge BC entirely rather than carrying it
over by default.

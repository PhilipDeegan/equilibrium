CONTEXT: eta_h (per-AMR-level regularization parameter) at level L5 was
calibrated (L1-L6 re-earn ladder 0, 0, 1e-5, 1e-7, 3e-9, 1e-10, 1e-12, last
touched 2026-07-30) against an old telescope checkpoint, and separately
project_odd27_amr_junction_leak.md (Part 25/36) concluded "eta_h is
negligible for the real spheromak" from that same-era testing.

WHAT FAILED: re-sweeping L5 directly against the real, properly-resolved
checkpoint (cells_across=220, /bk/l5_core_diameter9_cells_across_220.h5,
2026-08-08 night, L0-L4 held at the old re-earned ladder) showed eta_h does
real, non-negligible work: monotonic improvement from eta5=0 to 1e-8 (step3
growth 2.64% -> 2.04%, ~23% reduction), only saturating by eta5=1e-7/1e-6
(within 0.02% of the 1e-8 value). This directly contradicts the earlier
"eta_h negligible" reading.

ROOT CAUSE: the earlier calibration/conclusion was made against a telescope
with different resolution than the real spheromak checkpoint currently in
use. At cells_across=220 the curvature scale differs enough that eta_h has
a real effect that simply didn't show up under the old, coarser telescope.

LESSON: an eta_h (or similar per-level regularization/damping parameter)
calibration or negligibility conclusion is only valid for the specific
checkpoint resolution it was tested against. Before citing or reusing an
"eta_h is negligible" (or any similar knob-is-negligible) finding, check
whether it was tested against the currently-in-use production checkpoint,
not an older or differently-resolved stand-in telescope — results do not
transfer across resolution changes.

CONTEXT: Candidate fix for the L8 base-update blowup (H/H0=134): does
enable_ancestor_debias_correction (adaptive_lattice.hpp) resolve it?

WHAT FAILED: The debias correction is real and correctly targeted — it
fixes a genuine Jensen-inequality restriction bias in cross-level
ancestor substitution, closing ~82% of an isolated junction's
flux-isotropy error (verified at 1- and 2-level gaps), survives reflux,
and is zero-cost when inert. But applied reasoning/estimation showed its
effect on the real production spheromak IC is 4+ orders of magnitude too
small to move the L8 blowup.

ROOT CAUSE: The correction's effect is curvature-proportional, and the
real spheromak IC is flat almost everywhere except a tiny core — so
there's almost no curvature for it to act on at the scale that matters
for the L8 blowup. The blowup's real driver (confirmed separately) was
under-resolution: the calibrated production spheromak only had ~17.9
cells across its own diameter at L8, giving H/H0 ∝ cells_across^-1.85
scaling — a resolution problem, not a cross-level-bias problem.

LESSON: This is a correct fix for a real, different bug — it should
still ship on by default eventually (it fixes genuine per-junction
flux-isotropy error), but don't expect turning it on to change anything
for the L8 blowup specifically. Don't re-test it as a blowup fix without
new evidence the curvature regime has changed (e.g. a much less smooth
IC). The actual fix for under-resolution is more cells_across, not
better cross-level correction — see project_hop_telescope_resolved_spheromak
and project_odd27_amr_junction_leak.

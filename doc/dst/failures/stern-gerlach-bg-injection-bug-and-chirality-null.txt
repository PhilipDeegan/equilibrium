CONTEXT: stern_gerlach.py (spin/chirality deflection probe using a rotational-wake "SG magnet" construction) was crashing on every fresh checkout and, once fixed, needed its background-field injection mode checked for artifacts before trusting any chirality result from it.

WHAT FAILED (crash, fixed): --load defaulted to a nonexistent, oddly-specific snapshot file (proton_candidate_A_R40_IC.npz), apparently copied in from an unrelated exploration. Fixed: now builds its own spheromak IC when the snapshot file is missing, and the default filename is derived from --R/--amplitude/--lam-rings instead of a fixed arbitrary string.

WHAT FAILED (real bug found): the per-step "SG magnet" background injection (pa += _rot_bg*bg_z every raw step, no dt scaling, nothing ever removing it) blows up unconditionally — tot_circ grows ~7000x by step 3000 — REGARDLESS of cross_scale/lam_mode settings. An isolation sweep confirmed gradient=0 is stable/bounded, but any nonzero gradient blows up the same way whether step_cross/dynamic lam are on or off. This is the original/buggy "accumulate" mode, kept only for reference; a "once" mode (single injection at t=0, no re-injection) is the honest fix; a "rate" mode also exists.

WHAT FAILED (spurious result caught): under the buggy "rate" mode, spin+1 vs spin-1 circ_z_com trajectories showed an apparent "opposite deflection" (ratio≈-1.8), which could look like a real chirality signal. It does NOT hold up under scrutiny: tot_circ was still climbing throughout that run (not actually bounded, just slower than "accumulate"), and the two spins start from non-mirror-symmetric circ_z_com values — the sign flip is more likely a coincidental crossing during a shared decay-to-zero than two real opposite equilibria. Do not cite this ratio as evidence of chirality-dependent deflection.

RESULT once fixed (real null): under the honest "once" mode, spin+1 and spin-1 give nearly identical circ_z_com trajectories (ratio≈+1.01, not the expected ≈-1) — a clean null result: no chirality-dependent deflection from this rotational-wake construction. This is consistent with (not new evidence for or against) the already-known missing Ampère/Lorentz term in the theory (coupling a moving/winding lam/current into sourcing phi circulation) — not yet re-derived against equilibrium.substrate.curl's plaquette loops.

LESSON: before trusting any deflection/chirality asymmetry number out of a rolling background-field injection, verify tot_circ (or the equivalent driven quantity) is actually bounded over the full run, not just slower-growing than some other mode — an apparent sign-flip result can be a coincidental crossing of two still-diverging trajectories rather than two stable opposite equilibria. Also check that the two compared initial conditions are genuinely mirror-symmetric before comparing their trajectories as a chirality test.

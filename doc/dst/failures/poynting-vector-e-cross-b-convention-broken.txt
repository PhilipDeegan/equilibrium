CONTEXT: months of sessions tried to define a DST "Poynting vector" (energy flux) using the classical E:=-dphi/dt, B:=curl(phi), S:=ExB convention, to look for radiation-like signatures around knots/rings.

WHAT FAILED: every version of that convention failed, across all prior Poynting probe scripts. Only one of the four existing Poynting probe scripts (probe_poynting_step_only.py) was rebuilt on the correct mechanism and reverified; the other 3 still use the old E×B convention, and two of those also still drive via the separately-discredited step_cross(gate="barrier") mechanism. They were left as-is deliberately (Philip's call), not fixed.

ROOT CAUSE: diagnosed structurally, not just empirically. DST's (u at cell, phi on 14 faces) update is a scalar/acoustic-style staggered wave scheme, not full vector-EM. B=curl(phi) is a separate composite quantity that was never part of the local energy conservation law at all — there is no term in the real step() where an ExB-shaped quantity appears. The correct local energy flux, derived and verified directly against SubstrateLattice.step() (including nonuniform sigma), is S_i,k = W_k*phi[i,k]*ubar_i,k — a scalar flux per face using the face-averaged u, not a cross product of two vector fields. Residual against the real per-step energy change scales as clean O(dt^2) from dt=1e-3 to 1e-5, confirming this is the actual mechanism ExB was standing in for.

LESSON: do not build or trust any DST energy-flux/Poynting-style diagnostic on an E×B convention — it cannot work structurally in this scheme, regardless of gate or IC tuning. Use equilibrium/substrate/energy_flux.py (S_i,k formula, or local_energy_flux_vector for a projected 3-vector) instead. The 3 old, still-ExB-based Poynting probe scripts should not be trusted or extended until ported to the new module; two of them are doubly compromised by also using the discredited step_cross barrier gate. This finding is documented in full in doc/mems/project_dst_em_field_convention_crisis.md, whose "START HERE" status is now closed/resolved by this derivation — but the 3 unported scripts remain a live trap for anyone who reruns them expecting valid output.

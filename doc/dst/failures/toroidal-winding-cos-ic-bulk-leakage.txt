CONTEXT: init_toroidal_winding's cos/cos IC, checked for whether it behaves as a standing wave, a traveling wave, or something else, using probe_ring_standing_vs_traveling.py at two ring scales (N_ring=24 and N_ring=96), with sponge BC confirmed to rule out reflection artifacts.

WHAT FAILED: instantaneous du/dt at t=0 correlates 1.0 with sin(K*idx) — a traveling-wave signature — but tracked over a full period, u's own K-mode content collapses to near-zero within ~150-300 steps and never recovers, at both scales tested. Meanwhile phi's K-mode content (the curl-free-in-disguise pattern, per [[project_dst_discrete_curl]]) stays frozen at ~0.998-1.0 the entire run. The IC is neither a genuine standing wave nor a genuine traveling wave.

ROOT CAUSE: each ring tile has only 2 of its 14 faces on the ring path — the other 12 leak straight into the 3D bulk, and that leakage is most of what's actually happening to the pattern. Confirmed by a separate bare-bump-relaxation test on a closed ring (waterfall plot showing symmetric counter-propagating wavefronts meeting/reinforcing at the antipodal tile, with most energy gone into the bulk otherwise) — same bulk-leakage effect, independent construction.

Also attempted and not completed: building a genuine quadrature (cos+sin, one-directional rotation) companion IC as a cleaner ring construction — a by-hand dispersion derivation hit a sign inconsistency and was abandoned; the safer path identified but not yet taken is reusing the already-validated traveling-wave-reproducer formula (exp/2026/08/19/18_30_traveling_wave_reproducer/) adapted to ring geometry, rather than re-deriving the dispersion relation from scratch.

LESSON: don't treat init_toroidal_winding's cos/cos IC as either a clean standing-wave or traveling-wave ring object — with only 2/14 faces on-path, bulk leakage dominates its short-term evolution at both small and large ring scales, so any measurement assuming coherent ring propagation from this IC needs bulk-leakage accounted for first. Reusable tooling for this class of test: probe_ring_bump_relaxation.py (--save-npz), plot_ring_bump_relaxation.py, probe_ring_standing_vs_traveling.py (tracks both u and phi K-mode phase/amplitude, sponge-corrected).

# 2026-11-07

## Reference targets: CMS PAS BPH-24-005 (rare b -> s mu mu, Run 3)

Source: `BPH-24-005-pas.pdf` (repo root), CMS, 2026-08-20, 172 fb^-1 at 13.6 TeV.
Context: Sabine Hossenfelder post; LHCb reanalysis reports 4.3-4.8 sigma
(separate paper, not read). CMS gives no significance.

Measured vs interpreted:
- Measured: yields per q^2 bin, normalised to the X_b -> J/psi Y_s resonant mode.
- Interpreted: "below SM" is a distance to SM calculations that rest on form
  factors and charm loops (non-perturbative); the sigma is conditional on them.

Targets a substrate model would eventually need (not testable now: no stable
hadron in DST yet):
- psi(2S)/J/psi ratios (Table 7, x1e3), pure same-channel ratios, no SM normalisation:
  K+ 80.19+-0.57, K0S 78.69+-0.71, K*+ 63.62+-0.67, K*0 66.2+-1.0,
  phi 64.09+-0.46, Lambda 73.3+-1.2
- Isospin asymmetry A_I(q^2) compatible with zero for K and K*: rate independent
  of the spectator light quark.
- dB/dq^2 ordering vs theory: Bs->phi mu mu lowest (below all predictions),
  B->K mu mu at band edge, B->K* mu mu closest. Hides world-average B(J/psi Y_s)
  (2-22%), so not purely clean.
- The anomaly sits in the hadron-structure sector, the part the SM cannot calculate
  from first principles; this is where a bound-state ontology should compete.

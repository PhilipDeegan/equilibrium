## SESSION CONTINUED (2026-09-07, past midnight) — the direct phenomenology test run: (phi,psi) genuinely sources real B (not noise), but it's a trailing wake, not a co-propagating field — clean negative under the corrected criterion

`exp/2026/09/07/00_10_wave_plus_phi_psi_b_field/probe_wave_b_field.py`. Real traveling wave (`seed_traveling_wave`, curl-free), `(phi,psi)` coupling switched on, `B` measured with the HONEST operator (`equilibrium.substrate.curl.b_vec_vectorized`, validated against classical wire/loop fields — not the 12-triangle `circ_D` metric, which is contaminated by a wave's own sampling noise). `R=15, HALF_LEN=90, N=164,375`, 5000 steps, `c2=0` baseline vs `c2=1e-3` test.

- **Baseline (`c2=0`)**: `|B|_rms` stays at the machine-precision noise floor the ENTIRE run (8.97e-16 → 1.23e-15) — confirms the known frozen-B result exactly, and confirms `b_vec_vectorized` (unlike `circ_D`) has no wave-noise contamination problem.
- **Test (`c2=1e-3`)**: `|B|_rms` GROWS REAL AND LARGE — 2.2e-9 → 7.73e-4, five to six orders of magnitude above the baseline's noise floor by the end. **This is genuine, non-noise, honestly-measured curl generation — the clearest positive signal of the whole night.**
- **But it does NOT co-propagate.** `u_centroid_z` advances smoothly and monotonically the entire run (-46.6 → +38.0, tracking the wave's real motion at roughly `v≈3.3`, close to `v_phase=3.62`). `B_centroid_z` does NOT follow — after a brief initial co-drift (first ~300 steps), it reverses and stays confined to a narrow band (roughly -46 to -52) near the wave's ORIGINAL launch region for the rest of the run, while `u_centroid_z` moves ~85 units away. The gap grows from ~0 to -88 by the end.

**Diagnosis**: `(phi,psi)` deposits real curl into phi via `circ_D`/`scatter_DT` computed from wherever the wave's phi CURRENTLY is — as the wave transits through a region and moves on, the curl it leaves behind stays roughly pinned there (only locally redistributed by `(phi,psi)`'s own bounded-oscillation dynamics, per tonight's earlier finding that this coupling has no `u`-dependence and can't advect). So the mechanism produces a **trailing wake** of magnetization behind the wave, not a **co-moving field** that travels with it — qualitatively the wrong phenomenology for light, whose B travels WITH the wave, not left behind it.

**Verdict, under Philip's corrected acceptance criterion (judge candidate terms purely by whether they reproduce real magnetism's phenomenology, not by whether they feel derived)**: `(phi,psi)` FAILS this test — cleanly, not ambiguously. It is not a candidate mechanism for a genuine leapfrogging photon, regardless of its earlier-established stability/chirality properties. This is a real, decisive, informative negative result — the first time tonight's central question ("does the wave show a real, co-propagating B") has actually been asked and answered directly.

## (2026-08-23, compressed): discrete-floor-scale search — methodology and real-data anchors solid, absolute scale still open

**Goal**: find DST's discrete floor cell size from real measured data outward, not by assuming Planck length. Key reframing: the metre (fixed `c`, 1983 SI) and second (fixed atomic-transition count) are themselves emergent in DST — only ratios between same-kind measurements, ideally exact integer tick counts, count as real substrate content.

**Built `run/substrate/earth_moon_gradient_dst.py`** (unit-free, `fractions.Fraction`-based). Real structural finding: DST's throughput law `f=1-u` (rational polynomial) ALWAYS resolves to an exact integer tick pair for rational input; GR's Schwarzschild `f=sqrt(1-2u)` is generically irrational — no exact integer-tick representation except at special `u`. Real structural mismatch: GR can only be the continuum limit of some other rational-closed discrete law; DST's linear law is at least eligible in a way GR's isn't.

**`earth_moon_gradient.py` improved**: added real Galileo GSAT0201/0202 eccentric-orbit perigee/apogee rows (Delva et al. 2018, PRL 121:231101, GR confirmed to ~2.5e-5), replacing arbitrary guess rows for that range. Derived `u_core=1.5*u_surface` (uniform-sphere result) replaces the old hardcoded `CORE_SURFACE_GAIN_US_PER_DAY=30.0` placeholder (matches old guess to ~0.26%, cf. Uggerhoj et al. 2016). Columns re-anchored to Earth's core (local throughput minimum) instead of classical r->infinity convention.

**Multiplicand (absolute floor scale) still completely unconstrained**, same status `N_SAGA=2880` always had — every tick-differential this session (Galileo 33-in-608B, core/surface 197-in-566B, Skytree 29-in-588T) is a ratio only. Real evidence the floor sits below Planck granularity: using `N_SAGA=2880`, Earth's core-vs-void deficit is only ~3e-6 floor units (million times too small for even one tick — "no subtick" rule). Separately, a proton-structure argument gives a loose upper ceiling of ~8.4e-26 m (~5.2 billion times above Planck length) — consistent with, not contradicting, the lower bound. Large open range, nothing pins it down further.

**N-body softening-length literature (Power et al. 2003)**: useful methodological hint only (sweep-a-cutoff-and-compare technique) — not an importable formula (their softening shrinks toward zero with resolution; DST's floor is hypothesized fixed).

**Best real-data anchor: Takamoto et al. 2020 (Tokyo Skytree, Nature Photonics 14:411-415)** — two transportable Sr optical lattice clocks, 450m height difference, real measured beat frequency 21.1771(18) Hz against the Sr clock transition 429,228,004,229,873.1 Hz. Cleanest tick-differential computed all session (both numbers raw hardware measurements, no `G`-as-fudge-factor). Short-baseline near-surface comparisons are a MUCH tighter constraint than long-baseline orbital ones (Galileo) despite "worse" relative precision (9.1e-5 vs 2.48e-5) — because `|du/dr|` at Earth's surface is ~13.4x steeper than at Galileo's perigee. Caveat on trusting this to precision: see `doc/dst/failures/u-at-function-missing-sun-atmosphere-corrections.txt`.
